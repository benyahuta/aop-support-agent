# Larkspur & Pine customer policies (knowledge base)

## Returns
Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund to the original payment method. Return shipping is free with our prepaid label. Final-sale items (marked "Sample Sale") cannot be returned for change of mind.

## Damaged, defective, or wrong items
If something arrives damaged, defective, or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery and we'll refund the affected item. No need to send it back.

## Lost packages
If tracking hasn't updated for several days past the estimated delivery date, we treat the package as lost and refund or replace the affected items.

## Shipping
Orders ship within 2 business days. Standard shipping takes 3-6 business days and is free over $75; otherwise $7.95. Expedited (2-day) is $19.95. We ship to the US only.

## Cancellations and address changes
Orders can be cancelled or have their address changed until they ship. Once shipped, start a return after delivery.

## Warranty
Tents, packs, and jackets carry a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Warranty claims older than 30 days are handled by our specialist team.

## Contact
Human support is available 7am-7pm Pacific, Monday-Saturday. Our agents can hand you off any time.

## Price adjustments
We don't offer price matching or retroactive discounts on past orders.
